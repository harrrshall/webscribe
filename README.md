15. Powder metallurgy: production methods and basic steps

(a) Techniques for production of metal powders

Atomization (liquid atomization)

Water atomization or gas atomization: molten metal stream broken into droplets by high-velocity jets; droplets solidify to spherical (gas) or irregular (water) powders. Widely used for steels, stainless steels, copper, aluminum. Good control of particle size and purity.

Mechanical comminution (milling)

Crushing or ball milling of solid metal into powders. Used for brittle or refractory materials; produces irregular shapes; potential contamination.

Reduction of metal oxides

Chemical reduction (e.g., hydrogen reduction of iron oxide) produces sponge particles which are crushed and milled to size. Common for iron powders.

Electrolytic deposition

Metal is deposited from electrolyte and collected as powder (very pure, controlled morphology). Used for Cu, Ni, etc.

Chemical methods

Carbonyl process (e.g., Ni carbonyl) produces very pure spherical powders. Also chemical precipitation and thermal decomposition.

Vapor condensation / chemical vapor synthesis

Vapor phase nucleation and condensation produce extremely fine powders and special alloys.

Hydride-dehydride (for Ti)

Metal hydride formation, brittle hydride crushed and dehydrogenated to produce fine powders (used for Ti).

(b) Basic steps in Powder Metallurgy (PM) with neat sketches

Powder production (methods above) — obtain required particle size & shape.

Blending and mixing — powders mixed with lubricants and additives to ensure uniform composition and flow.

Compaction — powder placed in die and compacted by uniaxial or isostatic pressing to form a “green” compact (near-net shape). Green density typically 50–70% theoretical.

Sintering — heat the green compact below the melting point in controlled atmosphere to bond particles, reduce porosity and improve strength. Typical diffusion mechanisms cause neck growth between particles.

Secondary operations — forging, coining, infiltration, heat treatment, machining, sizing, impregnation or surface treatments to obtain final properties and dimensions.
